# Lumen Air Customer Service Policy (excerpt for the support assistant)

Each rule has a stable section id in square brackets. Cite these ids when you rely on a rule.

## Baggage

[BAG-1] Cabin baggage. Every passenger may bring one cabin bag up to 8 kg and 56 x 36 x 23 cm, plus one
personal item (handbag, laptop bag) that fits under the seat. Basic fares include the personal item only;
a cabin bag on a Basic fare costs $30 per flight when added online or $45 at the airport.

[BAG-2] Checked baggage allowance by fare. Basic: none included. Economy Standard: 1 bag up to 23 kg.
Economy Flex: 2 bags up to 23 kg each. Business: 2 bags up to 32 kg each. Elite Gold members receive one
extra 23 kg bag on any fare except Basic.

[BAG-3] Excess and overweight baggage. Bags between 23 kg and 32 kg cost $75 per bag per direction. Bags
over 32 kg are not accepted as checked baggage and must be shipped as cargo. Additional bags cost $90 each
per direction when added online, $120 at the airport.

[BAG-4] Delayed or damaged baggage. Customers must file a Property Irregularity Report at the airport or
online within 7 days (damage) or 21 days (delay). Interim expenses for essentials are reimbursed up to $50
per day for a maximum of 5 days with receipts.

## Changes and cancellations by the customer

[CHG-1] Changing a flight. Economy Flex and Business: free changes; the customer pays any fare difference.
Economy Standard: $75 change fee plus fare difference. Basic: changes not permitted.

[CHG-2] Same-day change. Elite members and Flex/Business fares may switch to an earlier or later flight on
the same day and same route for free, subject to availability, up to 2 hours before departure.

[CHG-3] Name corrections. Spelling corrections of up to 3 characters are free. Transferring a ticket to a
different person is not permitted.

## Refunds

[REF-1] Refundable fares. Economy Flex and Business fares are refundable to the original form of payment,
minus a $25 processing fee if cancelled less than 24 hours before departure.

[REF-2] Non-refundable fares. Basic and Economy Standard are non-refundable; taxes and airport charges are
always refunded on request. Any booking may be cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours of purchase if
departure is at least 7 days away.

[REF-3] Refund method. Refunds are issued only to the original form of payment, processed by the refunds
team within 7 business days. Lumen Air never refunds to a different account, never asks customers to
"approve" a transfer, and never requests card details or passwords by email.

## Delays and cancellations by Lumen Air

[DEL-1] Delays. For delays over 3 hours we provide meal vouchers; over 5 hours the customer may cancel for a
full refund. Overnight delays include hotel accommodation and transfers.

[DEL-2] Cancellations. If we cancel a flight the customer may choose rebooking on the next available Lumen
Air flight at no cost, rebooking on a partner airline when we cannot rebook within 24 hours, or a full refund.

[DEL-3] Compensation. Where the cause is within Lumen Air's control, compensation is $200 (under 1,500 km),
$400 (1,500 to 3,500 km) or $600 (over 3,500 km). Weather, air traffic control restrictions and security
events are outside our control and are not compensated, but care obligations under DEL-1 still apply.

[DEL-4] Irregular operations. During declared irregular operations (for example storms) customers on
affected flights may rebook free of charge onto any Lumen Air flight within 7 days of the original
departure, or request a full refund, without a change fee or fare difference.

## Payments

[PAY-1] Accepted payment methods: major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Lumen Air
vouchers. Vouchers are valid for 12 months and are not exchangeable for cash.

[PAY-2] Updating payment details. Agents and the assistant must never collect card numbers in chat, email
or phone. Card updates are made only through the secure payment link sent to the booking email. Refer to a
card only by its last four digits.

## Special assistance

[AST-1] Wheelchair assistance, travelling with medical equipment, and unaccompanied minors must be requested
at least 48 hours before departure. Service animals travel free in the cabin with documentation.

## Safety and wellbeing

[SAF-1] Injuries. If a customer reports an injury on board, at the gate or on a jet bridge, express care,
advise medical attention where appropriate, and escalate to the customer care team, who open an incident
report and contact the customer within 24 hours. Do not discuss liability.

[SAF-2] Harassment or discrimination reports are always escalated to the customer care team.

## Privacy

[PRV-1] Never disclose booking details to anyone other than the booking holder. Do not confirm whether a
named person is on a flight. Suspected phishing or fraud is reported to the fraud team.

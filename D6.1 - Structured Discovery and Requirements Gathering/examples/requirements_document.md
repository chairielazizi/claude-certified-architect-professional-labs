## 7. Conflicts and decisions needed

### C-1 - Human review before a letter is sent (severity: high)

| Stakeholder | Position | Normalized |
|---|---|---|
| Dana Whitfield (INT-01) | Auto-send simple letters; adjusters approve complex letters only | `auto_send_simple_letters` |
| Priya Raman (INT-02) | Draft yes, send no - a human approves every letter | `human_approval_every_letter` |

- **Decision owner:** Head of Compliance (regulatory veto) with the VP Claims Operations
- **Option to table:** Claude drafts every letter; adjusters approve routine letters with one click while edit rates are measured. Revisit auto-send only with evidence (e.g. 3 months of <1% material edits) and written Compliance sign-off.

### C-2 - Phase-1 scope: injury claims (medical data) (severity: high)

| Stakeholder | Position | Normalized |
|---|---|---|
| Dana Whitfield (INT-01) | Injury claims included from day one | `include_injury_claims` |
| Priya Raman (INT-02) | Exclude injury claims from phase 1 until controls are proven | `exclude_injury_claims` |

- **Decision owner:** Steering committee (sponsor, Compliance, Platform)
- **Option to table:** Phase 1 on property and auto claims; injury claims in phase 2 after a DPIA and a red-team of PHI handling. Use the overnight batch path for the injury backlog only once controls are proven.

### C-3 - Go-live date (severity: medium)

| Stakeholder | Position | Normalized |
|---|---|---|
| Dana Whitfield (INT-01) | Live by January 15 | `2027-01-15` |

- **Decision owner:** Executive sponsor, informed by the Platform delivery plan
- **Option to table:** Pilot with one adjuster team by January 15; general availability on March 1 after the security review.

### C-4 - AI usage budget (severity: medium)

| Stakeholder | Position | Normalized |
|---|---|---|

- **Decision owner:** Executive sponsor with Finance
- **Option to table:** Express the budget both ways: cost per letter and a monthly cap. Model the cost per letter in design (model choice, caching, batch for backlog) and agree which number wins.

### C-5 - Latency target (severity: low)

| Stakeholder | Position | Normalized |
|---|---|---|

## 11. Risks, assumptions and open questions

- **Risk** (INT-01): Low adoption if adjusters do not trust the drafts
- **Risk** (INT-02): Misdirected letter containing another customer's information (reportable breach)
- **Risk** (INT-03): Shared ClaimCore API rate limit (50 rps) could throttle drafting at peak
- **Risk** (INT-03): Prompt changes going live without testing
- **Assumption** (INT-03): The existing Claude enterprise agreement covers this use case; no new vendor review
- **Open question** (INT-01): Who owns the letter templates - Customer Experience or Legal?
- **Open question** (INT-02): How will demographic consistency of tone be measured, and by whom?
- **Open question** (INT-02): What is the field-error rate in today's manual letters?
- **Open question** (INT-03): Who on the business side owns the prompts and templates?

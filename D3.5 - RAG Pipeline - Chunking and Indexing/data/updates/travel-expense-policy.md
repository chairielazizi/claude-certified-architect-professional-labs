# Travel and Expense Policy

## Purpose and scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Northwind Systems who travel on company business or incur business expenses on the company's behalf. It explains how to book travel, which costs the company will cover, and how to get reimbursed. Managers are responsible for checking that the expenses submitted by their team are reasonable and comply with this policy. Finance owns this policy and publishes updates in the policy portal.

## Booking travel
All flights, hotels and rental cars must be booked through the corporate travel portal, TravelDesk, unless the portal cannot offer the required route. Bookings made outside TravelDesk need written approval from your manager before the trip.

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium economy may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 10 hours or when a medical accommodation has been approved by HR. Rail travel is preferred over flying for trips shorter than 4 hours door to door.

Hotels should be mid-range business properties within the nightly cap published in TravelDesk for each city. Stays above the cap require a short justification in the booking notes.

## Per diem and meals
Instead of itemizing every meal, travelers receive a daily per diem allowance that covers meals, snacks and tips. The domestic per diem is USD 65 per full travel day. The international per diem is USD 105 per full travel day, or the local rate published by Finance for high-cost cities such as London, Tokyo and Zurich. On the first and last day of a trip the per diem is paid at 75 percent.

Client entertainment is not covered by the per diem. Business meals with clients must be itemized, list every attendee and their company, and stay under USD 120 per person including tax and tip. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client meal.

## Receipts
Receipts are required for any single expense of USD 25 or more, and for all lodging and airfare regardless of amount. Digital photos of receipts uploaded to the expense tool are accepted; you do not need to keep the paper original. If a receipt is lost, complete Form EX-12 (Missing Receipt Declaration) and have it signed by your manager. More than three missing receipt declarations in a calendar year will trigger an audit of your expenses.

## Submitting expense reports
Submit your report in the Expensify tool within 30 days of the end of your trip or the date the cost was incurred. Reports submitted after 30 days require approval from your department VP and may be rejected. Each report should cover a single trip or a single calendar month of local costs.

Your manager approves the report first, then Finance reviews it. Approved reimbursements are paid by direct deposit in the next payroll run, normally within 10 business days of final approval.

## Corporate card
Employees who travel more than four times per year are issued a corporate card. The corporate card must be used for airfare and hotels whenever possible. Personal charges on the corporate card are not allowed; accidental personal charges must be repaid within 15 days. Card statements must still be reconciled in the expense tool every month.

## Non-reimbursable expenses
The company does not reimburse traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment such as movies or minibar items, upgrades purchased for personal comfort, travel insurance that duplicates the company policy, or costs for a spouse or family member who joins the trip.

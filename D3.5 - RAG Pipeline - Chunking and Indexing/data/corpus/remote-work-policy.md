# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Most roles at Northwind Systems are eligible for hybrid work. Fully remote arrangements require approval from your manager and HR and are reviewed every year. Roles that require on-site equipment, such as lab and facilities roles, are not eligible for fully remote work. New hires work on-site for at least two days per week during their first 90 days.

## Core hours and availability
All employees, wherever they work, must be available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours you may set a flexible schedule agreed with your manager. Keep your calendar and chat status up to date so teammates know when you are reachable.

## Home office equipment
Remote and hybrid employees can claim a one-time home office stipend of USD 500 for a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. Claims must be submitted with receipts within 6 months of becoming eligible. Company laptops, docks, and headsets are provided by IT and remain company property. An internet allowance of USD 40 per month is paid through payroll to fully remote employees.

## Workspace and security
Your home workspace should be quiet, private, and safe. Follow the Information Security Policy at home: lock your screen when you step away, keep company devices away from family members, and use the VPN on untrusted networks. Printing Confidential documents at home is not allowed.

## Working from another location
You may work temporarily from another location in your home country for up to 4 weeks per year. Working from another country requires prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax and immigration rules, and is limited to 20 working days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted at least 30 days before the planned start.

## Office attendance
Hybrid employees are expected in the office on team anchor days, usually two days per week chosen by each department. Desks are booked through the Spaces app. Travel between home and your regular office is a personal commuting cost and is not reimbursed by the company.

## Ending a remote arrangement
The company may end a remote arrangement with 30 days notice if business needs change or performance expectations are not met. Employees may also ask to return to a hybrid or on-site pattern at any time. Equipment must be returned to IT within 14 days of leaving the company.

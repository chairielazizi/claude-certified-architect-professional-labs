# Employee FAQ

## How do I check my PTO balance?
Log in to Workday and open the Time Off tile. Your available balance, pending requests, and accrual history are shown there.

## How much unused vacation can I carry over into next year?
You can carry over up to 10 unused vacation days into next year.

## Who do I contact if my laptop is lost?
Contact the IT service desk as soon as possible so they can lock the device remotely.

## Can I expense my home internet?
Fully remote employees receive a monthly internet allowance through payroll. Hybrid employees cannot expense home internet.

## How do I get a new monitor for working from home?
Use the one-time home office stipend described in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

## What is the meal allowance on business trips?
Travelers receive a daily per diem. See the Travel and Expense Policy for the current rates.

## How do I request new software?
Open a request in the IT service portal. IT reviews all software for security before it is purchased.

# Procurement and Purchasing Policy

## Purpose
This policy ensures that Northwind Systems buys goods and services at fair prices, from vetted vendors, with the right approvals. It applies to all purchases paid by the company, including software subscriptions, hardware, and professional services. Finance owns this policy.

## Approval thresholds
Purchases up to USD 5,000 can be approved by your direct manager. Purchases from USD 5,000 to USD 25,000 require approval from your department VP. Purchases above USD 25,000 require approval from the CFO and a competitive bid from at least three vendors. Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid an approval threshold is a policy violation.

## Purchase requests and purchase orders
Every purchase above USD 1,000 needs a purchase request in the Coupa procurement system before any commitment is made to the vendor. Once approved, Coupa issues a purchase order (PO) number that must appear on the vendor invoice. Invoices without a valid PO number are returned to the vendor unpaid.

## Vendor onboarding
New vendors must be onboarded before the first order. Submit Form PRC-7 (New Vendor Request) in Coupa with the vendor's tax ID, bank details, and a completed security questionnaire. Vendors that will process Restricted data must also pass a security review by the Security team and sign the data processing agreement. Onboarding normally takes 10 business days.

## Software and SaaS purchases
All software, including free trials and browser extensions that access company data, must be reviewed by IT before use. IT checks whether an approved tool already exists, confirms single sign-on support, and reviews data handling. Software renewals are managed centrally; do not renew a subscription on a personal or corporate card.

## Preferred vendors
Use preferred vendors listed in Coupa whenever possible. Preferred vendors have negotiated pricing and completed onboarding. If you choose a non-preferred vendor for a purchase above USD 5,000, document the reason in the purchase request. Preferred vendor contracts are reviewed annually by Finance.

## Conflicts of interest and gifts
Employees must disclose any personal relationship with a vendor before taking part in a purchasing decision. Gifts from vendors worth more than USD 75 must be declined or reported to Legal. Vendor-paid travel or event tickets always require Legal approval.

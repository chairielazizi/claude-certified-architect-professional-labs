# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

## Overview
Northwind Systems provides paid time off (PTO) so employees can rest, travel, and handle personal matters. PTO covers vacation and personal days; sick leave and parental leave are tracked separately. This policy applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## PTO accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month, which is 21 days per year. After five years of continuous service the accrual rate increases to 2.08 days per month, or 25 days per year. PTO starts accruing on your first day but can only be used after completing 60 days of employment. Your current balance is shown in the Workday self-service portal.

## Requesting time off
Submit PTO requests in Workday at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or more, and at least two working days in advance for shorter absences. Your manager should respond within three working days. During the year-end freeze from December 15 to December 31, finance and support teams need director approval for PTO.

## Carryover and payout
You may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1, so plan your time off accordingly. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year. Unused PTO is paid out on termination only where local law requires it.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick days can be used for your own illness, medical appointments, or to care for a sick family member. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement and can be split into up to two blocks. Notify HR at least 30 days before your expected leave start date where possible.

## Public holidays
The company observes 11 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar for each country is published in Workday every November. If you are required to work on a public holiday, you receive a replacement day off to be used within 90 days.

## Bereavement and other leave
Up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave are available on the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days for extended family. Jury duty and military service leave are paid as required by local law. Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted with VP approval and does not accrue PTO.

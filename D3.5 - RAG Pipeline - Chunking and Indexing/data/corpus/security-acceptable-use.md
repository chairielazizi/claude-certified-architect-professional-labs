# Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to everyone with access to Northwind Systems networks, devices, or data, including employees, contractors, and vendors. It sets the minimum security rules that protect customer data and company systems. Violations may lead to loss of access and disciplinary action.

## Accounts and passwords
Use a unique passphrase of at least 14 characters for your company account. Passwords no longer need to be rotated on a fixed schedule; you must change your password immediately if you suspect it has been exposed. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, the code repository, and every system that holds customer data. Hardware security keys are required for administrators. Never share your credentials, including with IT staff; IT will never ask for your password.

## Devices
Company laptops are managed by IT and must have full-disk encryption, automatic updates, and endpoint protection enabled. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email and chat only through the approved mobile management app. Do not install unapproved software; request new tools through the IT service portal.

## Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer personal data and payment data are Restricted. Restricted data must never be stored on personal devices, sent to personal email, or pasted into unapproved AI tools. Confidential and Restricted files may be shared externally only through the approved secure file transfer service.

## Reporting security incidents
Report any suspected security incident to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovering it. Incidents include a lost or stolen laptop or phone, a phishing email you clicked, or data sent to the wrong recipient. Report by calling the SOC hotline at extension 4400 or emailing soc@northwind.example. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete suspicious emails; use the Report Phishing button so the SOC can analyze them.

## Acceptable use
Company systems are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or break the law. Activity on company systems may be monitored for security purposes in line with local law. Using company systems for cryptocurrency mining, harassment, or running a private business is prohibited.

## Working in public places
When working in cafes, airports, or trains, use a privacy screen, connect only through the company VPN, and never leave your laptop unattended. Do not discuss Confidential or Restricted information where you can be overheard. Avoid public charging stations; use your own charger and power outlet.

## Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy require a written risk acceptance signed by the CISO. Exceptions are time-limited, recorded in the risk register, and reviewed every six months. Expired exceptions are revoked automatically.

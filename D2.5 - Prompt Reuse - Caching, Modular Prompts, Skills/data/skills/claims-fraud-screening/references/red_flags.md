# RED FLAG CATALOGUE (Northwind SIU, rev 2026-07)

| id | indicator | weight |
|---|---|---|
| RF-01 | Loss within 30 days of policy inception | 20 |
| RF-02 | Cover or sum insured increased within 60 days before the loss | 20 |
| RF-03 | High-value item (over $5,000) bought within 90 days of the loss | 10 |
| RF-04 | No receipts, photos or proof of ownership for items over $2,500 | 15 |
| RF-05 | Inconsistent accounts of the loss between statements | 15 |
| RF-06 | Claimant reluctant to provide documents or meet an adjuster | 10 |
| RF-07 | Two or more prior claims for similar items in 3 years | 15 |
| RF-08 | Police report missing or filed more than 48 hours after a theft | 10 |
| RF-09 | Damage pattern inconsistent with the reported cause | 20 |
| RF-10 | Financial distress disclosed or evident (arrears, recent cancellation) | 5 |

Notes:
- RF-01 and RF-02 are independent: both can apply.
- RF-03 requires the purchase date; if unknown, list it under information needed.
- RF-08 can only be assessed for theft or malicious damage claims.

# Subrogation demand letter

## When to use
Only when the other party is identified and liability is clear (handbook H7.4). If liability is disputed,
recommend a liability assessment instead of drafting.

## Steps
1. Collect: our claim id, date and place of loss, the at-fault party's insurer and policy number (mask all but
   the last four characters), amount paid, deductible paid by our customer.
2. State the facts in neutral language with dates; no adjectives about the other driver.
3. Demand the total of the amount paid plus the customer's deductible, itemised.
4. Give a 30-day response deadline and the remittance reference.
5. Attach: proof of payment, repair invoice, police or incident reference.

## Tone
Formal, factual, concise. One page. Never threaten litigation in the first letter.

## Template skeleton
Subject: Subrogation demand - our claim <id> - your insured <masked policy>
Paragraph 1: facts. Paragraph 2: liability basis. Paragraph 3: itemised demand. Paragraph 4: deadline and
remittance details. Sign-off: Recovery Unit, Northwind Mutual.

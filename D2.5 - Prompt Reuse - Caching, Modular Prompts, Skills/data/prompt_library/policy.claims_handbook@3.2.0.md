# Northwind Mutual Claims Handling Handbook (v3.2)

## H1 Scope and authority
H1.1 This handbook governs first-notice-of-loss (FNOL) triage for personal property (Home, Contents, Landlord)
and motor (Motor Private, Motor Fleet) policies, and for Small Business Property.
H1.2 Triage recommendations are approve, refer or deny. Approve means the claim may be paid by a handler within
their authority. Refer means a senior handler, specialist or investigator must review. Deny means the loss is
clearly outside cover under a cited section.
H1.3 Authority limits: standard handlers may approve up to $15,000; senior handlers up to $75,000; anything
above $75,000, any bodily injury, and any claim with a fraud score of 40 or more is a referral.
H1.4 A recommendation must cite at least one handbook section and, where money is involved, the applicable
coverage schedule row (region and product).

## H2 Notification and time limits
H2.1 Claims must be notified within 30 days of the loss for property and 7 days for motor. Late notification
is not an automatic denial: refer when the delay prejudiced our ability to investigate (for example, repairs
already completed with no photos).
H2.2 Theft and malicious damage must be reported to police within 48 hours; the police reference is required
before approval.
H2.3 For escape of water, the policyholder must take reasonable steps to stop the leak and dry the property.
Secondary damage caused by unreasonable delay is excluded under H6.4.

## H3 Verification
H3.1 Confirm the policy was in force on the date of loss and that the premium was paid. Losses in the first 14
days of a new policy are always referred for verification.
H3.2 Confirm the insured property or vehicle matches the schedule. Unlisted high-value items over $2,500 under
Contents are covered only up to $2,500 each unless separately specified.
H3.3 For motor claims confirm the driver was named or within the any-driver clause, held a valid licence, and
was not under the influence; any doubt is a referral.

## H4 Deductibles and limits
H4.1 The deductible for each claim is taken from the coverage schedule for the policy's rating region and
product, unless the policy schedule states a voluntary higher deductible.
H4.2 Catastrophe (CAT) events declared by Northwind apply the CAT surge factor from the schedule to the
deductible, capped at twice the standard deductible.
H4.3 The limit in the schedule is the maximum per claim. Claims whose estimate exceeds 80% of the limit are
referred for a site inspection before approval.
H4.4 Contents claims for electronics older than 5 years are settled at depreciated value: 15% per year from
purchase, minimum 25% of replacement cost.

## H5 Covered perils (property)
H5.1 Fire, smoke, lightning and explosion.
H5.2 Storm and flood, except damage to gates, fences and hedges, and except flood where the property is in a
zone the schedule marks as flood-excluded.
H5.3 Escape of water or oil from fixed installations, including the cost of tracing the leak up to $5,000.
H5.4 Theft or attempted theft, involving forcible and violent entry for unoccupied properties.
H5.5 Subsidence, heave and landslip, subject to a separate deductible of $1,500.
H5.6 Accidental damage only where the policy includes the accidental damage extension.

## H6 Exclusions (property)
H6.1 Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rot, mould and pests.
H6.2 Loss while the property was unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days, except fire and lightning.
H6.3 Deliberate acts by the policyholder or members of the household.
H6.4 Secondary damage caused by failing to mitigate a loss once discovered.
H6.5 Business use of a residential property not declared to us.

## H7 Motor specifics
H7.1 Comprehensive cover pays own-vehicle damage; third-party-only does not.
H7.2 Windscreen claims up to $1,200 are approved without the deductible when repaired at an approved partner.
H7.3 Total-loss settlements use the market value on the date of loss from the approved valuation guide.
H7.4 Where the other party is clearly at fault and identified, flag the claim for subrogation recovery.

## H8 Small Business Property
H8.1 Stock is covered at cost price, not selling price.
H8.2 Business interruption requires the business interruption extension and is always referred.
H8.3 Equipment breakdown is excluded unless the engineering extension is in force.

## H9 Fraud indicators and referral
H9.1 Indicators include: loss shortly after inception or a cover increase, inconsistent accounts, reluctance to
provide documents, recently purchased high-value items without receipts, and prior claims for similar items.
H9.2 Triage computes a fraud score using the fraud-screening procedure. A score of 40 or more is referred to the
Special Investigations Unit (SIU); never tell the claimant a claim was referred for suspected fraud.

## H10 Communication standards
H10.1 Recommendations go to handlers, not claimants. Keep them factual and cite sections.
H10.2 When information is missing, list exactly what is needed (for example, police reference, photos,
receipts, repair estimate) instead of guessing.
H10.3 Never promise a payment amount before approval within authority.

# Acme Audio Customer Policies

## Returns
You can return most products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if they are unused and in the original packaging. Opened items that are not defective can still be returned within 30 days, but a 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund. Earbuds can only be returned if the hygiene seal is intact. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item.

## Warranty
Every product carries a limited warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty period is listed per product in the catalog (warranty_months) and starts on the order date. The warranty covers failures under normal use such as a battery that no longer charges, a broken hinge, or audio dropouts. It does not cover accidental damage, loss, or theft. Water damage is covered only for products rated waterproof. To make a warranty claim, open a support ticket with your order number as proof of purchase; we repair or replace the product, or refund it if neither is possible.

## Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over USD 75 within North America and takes 3-5 business days. International orders ship in 7-14 business days and include a surcharge based on the destination zone. Express shipping is available in North America and Europe for USD 15. Orders placed before 14:00 local warehouse time ship the same day.

## Price match
If you find a lower price from an authorized retailer within 14 days of purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference. Marketplace sellers and clearance sales are excluded.

## Support and accessibility
Support is available by chat and email 7 days a week. Customers with accessibility needs can request large-print manuals and phone support.

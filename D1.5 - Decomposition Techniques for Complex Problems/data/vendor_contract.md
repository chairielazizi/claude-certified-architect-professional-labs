# Master Services Agreement - Vendor X AI Support Platform

## 1. Definitions
"Customer" means the subscribing company. "Vendor" means Vendor X Ltd. "Services" means the hosted AI support platform, its APIs and related support. "Monthly Fee" means the subscription fee invoiced for a calendar month. "Personal Data" has the meaning given in the GDPR. "Incident" means any unplanned interruption or degradation of the Services. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. Services
Vendor shall provide the Services described in the Order Form, including the ticket-classification, reply-drafting and analytics modules. Customer shall provide Vendor with API access to its ticketing system and nominate an administrator. Vendor may update the Services from time to time provided that updates do not materially reduce functionality. Customer is responsible for configuring routing rules and for the content of its knowledge base.

## 3. Service levels
Vendor shall make the Services available 99.9% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 business days in advance. Vendor shall respond to Priority 1 Incidents within 1 hour, 24 hours a day. If monthly availability falls below 99.9%, Customer is entitled to a service credit of 10% of the Monthly Fee for each full 0.1% below the target, up to 50% of the Monthly Fee. Service credits are Customer's sole remedy for availability failures.

## 4. Data protection
Vendor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer and only within data centres located in the European Union. Vendor shall ensure that personnel authorised to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality, shall maintain a record of processing activities, and shall assist Customer with data subject requests and data protection impact assessments at no additional charge. Sub-processors may be engaged only with prior written notice of at least 30 days, during which Customer may object on reasonable grounds.

Vendor shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach. The notification shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed. Vendor shall cooperate with Customer's investigation and shall not inform any third party of the breach without Customer's consent unless required by law.

## 5. Fees and payment
Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments bear interest at 1% per month from the due date until paid. Fees are exclusive of VAT. Vendor may increase fees once per contract year by giving 60 days' written notice, capped at 5% per year.

## 6. Security
Vendor shall maintain ISO 27001 certification throughout the term. Vendor shall commission an independent penetration test at least annually and share an executive summary with Customer within 30 days of completion. Vendor shall remediate critical vulnerabilities within 14 days of discovery and high vulnerabilities within 30 days.

## 7. Term and termination
The initial term is 36 months. Either party may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not cured within 30 days of notice. Upon termination, Vendor shall return or delete all Customer data within 30 days and certify deletion in writing.

## 8. Liability
Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim. The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality, data protection obligations or indemnities. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

# Receipts checklist (level-3 resource: loaded only when filing a report)

- Receipt required for any single expense of USD 25 or more, and always for lodging and airfare.
- Itemized receipts for meals; card slips alone are rejected.
- Foreign-currency receipts: keep the original currency; the system converts at the corporate rate.
- Missing receipt: complete the Missing Receipt Declaration and have your manager sign it.
- More than three missing-receipt declarations per year trigger an audit.
